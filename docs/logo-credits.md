# Logo credits

The map shows a small logo on the card of 28 languages, only to identify the language being described. The logos are single-color renderings from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.34.0 (icon data CC0). The marks belong to their owners. This project is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of them.

All other languages show their initials. Five logos that Simple Icons provides were left out after a check of the owners' published policies: Go, Scratch, Delphi, Mathematica and Cypher (Neo4j). Their owners require permission for logo use, or publish no usage policy.

If you own one of these marks and want its logo removed or credited differently, please open an issue.
